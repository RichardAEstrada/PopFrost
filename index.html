A few little things

Good to know.

Is this a confirmed order?+

Not yet. We’re collecting interest to plan our launch. We’ll contact you personally to discuss the design, date, and final price. There’s no payment or purchase commitment when you submit the form.

How does the repeat-stamp discount work?+

We create your custom stamp once and keep it for future orders. When you reuse the same design, you can request a discounted price per dozen. Include your existing stamp or previous order reference; we’ll confirm your discount individually before your next order.

Can I request a custom design or business logo?+

Yes. Tell us your idea in the form, and we’ll review it with you. Please describe the artwork for now. We’ll ask for any files directly when we follow up, then confirm the details and any additional charges.

What about pickup and delivery?+

Pickup and delivery options depend on availability. Delivery may cost extra. We’ll confirm the arrangements personally before you decide to place an order.
